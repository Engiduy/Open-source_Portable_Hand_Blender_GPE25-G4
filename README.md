## 1\. About the Team

We are **Group 4 GPE25**, a student project team in the **Global Production Engineering (GPE)** program. This project brings together product development, engineering design, manufacturing planning, assembly, documentation, and teamwork.

Our goal is to apply engineering methods to a practical consumer product and share the resulting work in a structured, accessible format that others can study, reproduce, or improve.

## 2\. Project Purpose

The **Portable Hand Blender** project aims to develop a compact, handheld blending prototype for small-scale food preparation while applying the knowledge and skills gained through the GPE learning program.

The main project objectives are to:

* Translate user needs into product requirements and functional specifications.  
* Develop a practical product concept and corresponding mechanical and electrical designs.  
* Consider manufacturability, assembly, component selection, cost, and basic safety throughout product development.  
* Build and evaluate a prototype against the agreed project requirements.  
* Document the design and development process so that the work can be reviewed, reproduced, and further improved.  
* Practice cross-functional collaboration and open-source knowledge sharing.

## 3\. Product Overview

The **Portable Hand Blender** is a handheld kitchen-device concept intended to blend or mix suitable food ingredients in small quantities. Its basic operating principle is to transfer rotational motion from a motor through a drive mechanism to a blending attachment.

The product is being developed with attention to portability, ease of handling, straightforward assembly, and practical component selection. The final design, technical ratings, and suitability for particular ingredients should be verified against the released product documentation and prototype test results.

### 3.1 Main Functions

| Function | Description |
| :---- | :---- |
| **Blending / mixing** | Rotates a blending attachment to process suitable soft ingredients or liquids, subject to prototype capabilities. |
| **Handheld operation** | Allows the user to position and control the device manually during use. |
| **Motor activation and control** | Uses an electrical control interface to start and stop the motor; the exact control arrangement depends on the released design. |
| **Component assembly and maintenance** | Provides a product structure that can be assembled and inspected using the accompanying documentation. |

### 3.2 Key Design Considerations

* **Functionality:** Ability to perform the intended blending and mixing tasks.  
* **Usability:** Comfortable handling and a clear operating procedure.  
* **Safety:** Appropriate electrical, mechanical, and food-contact precautions.  
* **Manufacturability:** Feasible parts and processes for an academic prototype.  
* **Serviceability:** Access to components for inspection, repair, or replacement where the design permits.

## 5\. Basic Build Instructions

These instructions provide an overview only. **Do not begin fabrication or electrical assembly until the released design, BOM, detailed assembly guide, and safety checks are available.**

1. **Review the product documentation.** Confirm the intended function, applicable dimensions, specifications, and limitations.  
2. **Review the BOM and design files.** Identify which parts must be fabricated and which components must be purchased. Confirm mechanical and electrical compatibility.  
3. **Fabricate the required parts.** Follow the manufacturing instructions for the actual selected processes and materials.  
4. **Prepare the electrical components.** Refer to the electronics documentation and confirm the correct power source, connections, insulation, and switching arrangement.  
5. **Assemble the device.** Follow the specified sequence in the assembly guide; ensure that moving parts and fasteners are properly secured.  
6. **Inspect and test the prototype.** Check the assembly and electrical safety before performing controlled functional tests according to the approved test procedure.

**Important:** Exact tools, component ratings, wiring steps, fastening methods, and acceptance criteria must be taken from the finalized technical documents—not inferred from this README.

## 6\. Basic Use Instructions

The following is a preliminary usage outline and must be updated after prototype validation.

1. Inspect the device for loose parts, damaged components, or exposed wiring. Do not operate a damaged prototype.  
2. Confirm that the blending attachment is correctly installed and that the power arrangement matches the released technical documentation.  
3. Place the appropriate ingredients in a suitable container within the device's verified operating limits.  
4. Keep hands, utensils, loose clothing, and other objects away from moving parts. Operate the device only according to the validated control procedure.  
5. Stop the motor completely and isolate the power source before inspection, cleaning, disassembly, or adjustments.  
6. Clean only the components and surfaces approved for cleaning in the final product instructions. Do not immerse electrical parts unless specifically designed and verified for immersion.

**Safety limitation:** Do not assume the prototype is food-safe, waterproof, suitable for hot liquids, or safe for unrestricted household use without corresponding material checks and functional/safety validation.
